**Friends of the Tuolumne County Library Book Sellers Event**

**Saturday, September 12, 2026, 10 a.m.–2 p.m.**

The Friends of the Tuolumne County Library (FOTCL) and the Tuolumne County Library are hosting a FREE event (no charge to book sellers). Lunch and bottled water will be provided.

If you are interested in participating or have any questions, please contact Wendy Ledger at <Wendy@votype.com>. Space is limited, so please check in with Wendy to see if there are still spots available.
