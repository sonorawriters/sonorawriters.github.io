**The Boo-etry — A Spooky, Ghostly, Eerie Celebration**

**Friday, October 30, 2026, 7–9 p.m.**

- Blanche Abrams to read Poe's *The Raven*
- Featured guests to perform either famous literary or poetic works or their own poetry and prose. If you'd like to be a featured guest, submissions should be emailed to <amy.luning@gmail.com> or <blanchelabrams@gmail.com>
- Open mic for original poetry/prose
- This event is for adults and older kids ten and up
- There will be candy and treats, but no trick-or-treating
- This is a Halloween-centric event
- Costumes? Yes please. I shall dust off my bat wings
- Adults $5, Kids $3 — at the door
